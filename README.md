# AI-Powered Meal Planning & Automated Grocery Shopping

**Independent AI Product Management Case Study**

## Overview

What if Instacart could help customers decide what to cook, not just where to buy groceries?

Instacart SmartMeal is a conceptual AI-powered feature that generates personalized weekly meal plans and automatically converts ingredients into grocery carts.

The goal is to simplify grocery shopping, reduce meal-planning friction, and encourage long-term customer retention.

## Problem

Grocery shoppers often struggle with:
- Deciding what to cook each week
- Planning meals within a budget
- Finding recipes that match dietary preferences
- Manually adding ingredients to grocery carts
- Reducing food waste

## Proposed Solution

Instacart SmartMeal introduces:

- AI-generated weekly meal plans
- Personalized dietary and budget preferences
- Automatic ingredient-to-cart conversion
- Meal customization
- Integration with existing grocery checkout and delivery

## Product Strategy

The case study explores:

- Customer problems and hypothesized user segments
- Marketplace considerations
- Product opportunities and trade-offs
- MVP features and user stories
- Product prioritization
- Phased rollout strategy
- Success metrics and guardrails

## Success Metrics

**Primary Metric:** 8-week customer retention compared with a control group.

Supporting metrics include:
- Meal plan-to-cart conversion
- SmartMeal adoption
- Checkout conversion
- Repeat purchases
- Ingredient matching accuracy

These are proposed metrics, not results from a launched experiment.
